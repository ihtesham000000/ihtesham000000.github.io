BMI Calculator — Check Your Body Mass Index

Check your Body Mass Index in seconds. Enter your height and weight to see your BMI value, category (underweight, normal, overweight or obese) and the healthy weight range for your height. Free, private, works entirely in your browser.

How to use BMI Calculator

  1. Enter your height in centimeters
  2. Enter your weight in kilograms
  3. Click Calculate to see your BMI and healthy range

Frequently asked questions

What is a normal BMI?

A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered normal/healthy.

Is this BMI calculator accurate?

It uses the standard WHO formula. For athletes and pregnant women, BMI may be less meaningful.

Does it store my health data?

No — the calculation happens on your device and nothing is saved.
