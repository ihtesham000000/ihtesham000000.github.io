Pomodoro Timer — Study Smarter

Work with the proven Pomodoro method: 25 minutes of deep focus, then a 5-minute break, repeating in cycles. Track your completed focus sessions and build an unbreakable study or work routine. Free, no signup, works on mobile.

How to use Pomodoro Timer

  1. Press Start to begin a 25-minute focus session
  2. When the timer rings, take the 5-minute break
  3. Repeat the cycle — the counter tracks your completed sessions

Frequently asked questions

What is the Pomodoro technique?

A productivity method: 25 minutes of focused work, a 5-minute break, and a longer break after four cycles.

Can I change the durations?

This version uses the classic 25/5 timings; custom durations may be added later.

Is it free?

Yes — unlimited sessions.
