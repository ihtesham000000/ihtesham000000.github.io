Compress Image to KB — Exact Size, Free

Need your photo under 50KB for a job application or exam form? This compressor shrinks your image to the exact KB size you need, automatically adjusting quality and dimensions until your target is reached. No Photoshop, no software — everything on your phone.

How to use Image Compressor

  1. Upload the photo you need to compress
  2. Enter your target size in KB (e.g. 50, 20 or 100)
  3. Click Compress, then download your perfectly sized image

Frequently asked questions

How do I compress a photo to 50KB?

Upload your photo, type 50 as the target, and press Compress Image. The tool adjusts quality automatically until the file is under 50KB.

Is it free for government job forms?

Yes — completely free, unlimited use. Ideal for PPSC, FPSC, NTS and university admission forms.

Does compression hurt quality?

The tool finds the best quality that fits your target size, keeping your photo as sharp as possible.
