Word Counter — Words, Characters & More

Count words, characters, sentences and estimated reading time instantly as you type or paste. The essential free tool for students checking assignment limits, writers hitting word counts, and bloggers optimizing articles. No signup, no text stored.

How to use Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the box
  2. See live counts: words, characters, sentences, reading time
  3. Copy your text when done — nothing is uploaded anywhere

Frequently asked questions

Does it count characters with and without spaces?

Yes — you get both the total character count and a count excluding spaces.

How is reading time calculated?

At an average adult reading speed of about 200 words per minute.

Is my text private?

Yes — counting happens fully in your browser; your text never leaves your device.
