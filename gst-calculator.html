GST / Sales Tax Calculator

Calculate GST or sales tax in one tap. Add tax to a base price to get the total, or remove tax from a tax-inclusive amount to find the original price. Includes standard rates for Pakistan (17%), India (18%) and more — or enter any custom rate.

How to use GST Calculator

  1. Enter the amount
  2. Choose your tax rate (17% for Pakistan GST, or custom)
  3. Pick add tax or remove tax — see the breakdown instantly

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove GST from a total?

Choose remove tax mode: base price = total ÷ (1 + rate/100). The tool shows both parts.

What is Pakistan GST rate?

Standard GST in Pakistan is 17% — pre-selected in this calculator.
