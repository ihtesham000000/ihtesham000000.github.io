Frequently asked questions

What is the nisab for Zakat?

The minimum wealth before Zakat is due — the value of 87.48g of gold or 612.36g of silver, whichever is lower.

What percentage is Zakat?

2.5% of zakatable wealth held above the nisab for one lunar year.

Are debts deducted before Zakat?

Yes — short-term debts are subtracted before calculating.
